    The five dimensions

    • Diagnosis: seeing the real problem quickly.
    • Decisions under pressure: acting without complete information, and correcting course.
    • Communication: candour, consistency and calm under scrutiny.
    • Coalition building: sponsors, allies and the ability to win over sceptics.
    • Personal resilience: the support and habits that sustain you through a long, hard role.

    This is a self-reflection tool, not a validated psychometric test. Use it to decide where to invest before you start, ideally with a coach or trusted colleague.

    Questions

    Is this a psychometric test?

    No. It is a structured self-reflection based on common themes in crisis leadership. It is not validated as a psychological assessment.

    What is a good score?

    There is no pass mark. Look at the spread: your two lowest dimensions are where support or preparation will help most.

    Can my team use it?

    Yes. Many leaders find it useful to compare their own answers with how a trusted colleague would rate them.
