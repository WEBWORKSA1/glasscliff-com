      How the score works

      Each answer is scored from 0 (no risk) to 10 (high risk) and weighted by how strongly it relates to the conditions that research and practice link to failed crisis appointments. The weights are highest for the organisation's condition, a written mandate, resources and sponsorship. The total is scaled to 0–100.

      ScoreBandWhat it means
      0–29Solid groundWell supported. Confirm the essentials in writing.
      30–49Watch the edgeReal but negotiable risks.
      50–69Glass cliffHigh risk of being set up to fail without changes.
      70–100Free fallThe conditions for success are missing.

      The score describes the role, not you. A high score does not mean you would fail; it means the role, as offered, makes success harder than it needs to be. Read twelve red flags in a leadership offer for the reasoning behind each question.

      Questions

      Is my data stored?

      Your answers are saved only in your own browser so you can come back to them. Nothing is sent unless you use the email form.

      Can I use this for a role I already hold?

      Yes. Answer as things stand today. A high score suggests what to renegotiate with your board or sponsor.

      What should I do with a high score?

      Use the 'What to negotiate' list as the agenda for your next conversation, build a full list with the Offer Protection Builder, or request a confidential review.
