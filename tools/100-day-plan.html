Turnaround 100-Day Planner

Choose your situation and get a three-phase plan, dated from your start day, with weekly checkpoints. Print it or save it as a PDF.

Your 100-day plan

Why three phases

Crisis roles reward leaders who resist the urge to announce a plan in week one. Diagnosing first protects you from committing to the wrong priorities, and recording the position you inherited means you will be judged on progress rather than the starting point.

The plan combines actions that apply to almost every turnaround with actions specific to the situation you chose. Adapt it; it is a scaffold, not a script. The full reasoning is in The first 100 days of a turnaround role.

Questions

Can I edit the plan?

Print or save it as a PDF, then edit your copy. Your choices are remembered on this device.

Does this work for a role I already started?

Yes. Start from the phase you are in and use the earlier phases as a checklist of anything you skipped.

What if my situation is a mix of types?

Pick the dominant one, then run the planner again for the second and merge the lists.
