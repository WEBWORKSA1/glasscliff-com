Free tools for high-stakes roles

Five tools built on glass cliff research and governance practice. They run entirely in your browser, save your progress on your device, and send nothing unless you choose to.

Glass Cliff Risk Scanner

Twelve questions about a role you are weighing. Get a 0–100 risk score, the red flags and what to negotiate.

Free, about 4 minutes

Offer Protection Builder

Tick the risks in your offer and get a prioritised list of protections to ask for, ready to copy or print.

Free, about 2 minutes

Turnaround 100-Day Planner

Choose your situation and get a three-phase plan with weekly checkpoints, dated from your start day.

Free, about 3 minutes

Crisis Leadership Readiness Check

Fifteen statements across five dimensions. See where you are strong and what to build before you start.

Free, about 5 minutes

Board Appointment Check

For chairs and search committees: twelve checks that show whether you are setting your next leader up to succeed.

Free, about 3 minutes

Which tool should I use?

  • Offered a role? Start with the Risk Scanner, then build your negotiation list with the Offer Protection Builder.
  • Starting soon? Generate a plan with the 100-Day Planner and check yourself with the Readiness Check.
  • On a board or panel? Run the Board Appointment Check before you make the offer.

How the tools were built

Each tool turns findings from the research on the glass cliff (see our Research Library) and common governance practice into structured questions. The scores are indicators to guide a conversation, not predictions about any role or person.
