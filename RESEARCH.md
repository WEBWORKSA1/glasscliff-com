# Glasscliff.com — Competitive and Market Research

Research date: November 2026. 39 sites reviewed (two more, Fairygodboss and Verywell Mind, refused automated access).

## Why this concept

- **"Glass cliff" is a recognised research term with a living debate.** Coined by Michelle Ryan and Alex Haslam (Exeter, 2005); still producing new studies in 2025 (Coventry's FTSE 350 chair study, a US study finding no clear effect, a 2025 DCU thesis on ethnic-minority leaders, ANU keynote coverage). Evergreen informational demand plus a stream of news hooks.
- **The commercial neighbourhood pays well.** US CPCs: "executive leadership programs" ~$21, "executive leader development program" ~$22, "leadership development courses" ~$17, "executive coaching programs" ~$14 (seodata.dev / seojuice, 2026). Coaching and executive education advertisers bid on exactly the audience this site attracts.
- **Nobody owns the practical angle.** Existing glass cliff pages are definitions (Wikipedia, MasterClass, HR glossaries), opinion pieces, member-only webinars (Catalyst) or podcasts. No site helps a leader *evaluate a specific offer* or helps a board *appoint safely*. That gap is the lead-generation engine.

## Steal list (implemented)

1. Tool-first hero with an interactive "edge test" (Korn Ferry, Levels.fyi).
2. Score → negotiation asks → "get a confidential review" (Levels.fyi negotiation funnel).
3. Multi-step lead form with progress bar and confidentiality promise (CCL, Chief).
4. Two-audience split: individuals vs organisations (Russell Reynolds, CCL).
5. Advisor Network with tiered listing plans (The Muse, Torch).
6. Self-assessment quiz with profile output (Women on Boards, MindTools).
7. Board-facing checklist (Spencer Stuart, NACD).
8. Annotated research library with timeline (Wikipedia, Catalyst).
9. Glossary page (Lean In glossary, HR glossaries).
10. Survey participation for a recurring report (Women in the Workplace).
11. Monthly + one-time donations with transparent use of funds (AnitaB, 50/50 WOB).
12. Quarterly prizes with countdowns and a sponsor slot (Great Place To Work lists, Edge Prize).
13. Careers + talent pool (PowerToFly, Idealist).
14. Advertise page with packages and labelled sponsor content (HBR, Forbes, Glass Hammer).
15. Newsletter capture in footer and after guides (HBR, Lean In).
16. FAQ accordions with schema (MindTools).
17. Printable plans as lead magnets (Harvard PON).
18. Auto-updated news stream (DiversityInc, Korn Ferry "This Week in Leadership").
19. Video library with privacy-enhanced embeds (TED, Lean In).
20. Clear content-type tags (McKinsey).
21. Sticky mobile CTA (Ladders, Levels.fyi banners).
22. Owner/partnership bar (site owner requirement).
23. Search across guides, tools and glossary (HBR, SHRM).
24. Breadcrumbs and related reading (HBR).
25. Reading time and last-updated stamps (HBR, Forbes).
26. Cookie notice and full privacy page (all).
