Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

Summary

Our tools run in your browser and do not send your answers anywhere. When you submit a form, we receive what you typed so we can respond. We use cookies for basic site functions and Google uses cookies to serve and measure ads.

Information we collect

  • Form submissions (name, email, and what you choose to tell us), delivered to our team by email through the FormSubmit service.
  • Basic attribution attached to forms: the page you submitted from, the first page you visited, the referring site and any campaign (UTM) tags.
  • Tool answers saved only in your own browser's local storage so you can return to them. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser data.

How we use it

Only to respond to you, provide the service you asked for (for example, a confidential review or a prize entry), and improve the site. With your consent, we may share the details of a review request with the adviser we match you with. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Third-party services

  • Google AdSense (advertising) and Google Fonts (typefaces).
  • FormSubmit (form delivery).
  • YouTube and TED (video playback, only after you press play; YouTube runs in privacy-enhanced mode).
  • GitHub Pages (hosting).

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete information you have sent us. Use the contact form and choose "Privacy request". We keep form submissions only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping, and no longer than 24 months.

Children

Glasscliff is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
