The Glass Cliff Pulse Survey

Have you been offered or held a leadership role in difficult conditions? Ten anonymous questions. Results feed the Glass Cliff Pulse report.

Why we run this survey

Most glass cliff research uses company records or lab experiments. Far less captures what leaders themselves were offered, what they negotiated, and how it turned out. The Pulse fills that gap.

Your privacy

  • No name required. Email is optional and used only to send you the report.
  • Results are published only in aggregate.
  • Open-text answers may be quoted anonymously.
