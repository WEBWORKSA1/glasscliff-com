The Edge Prize

Quarterly prizes for the best writing on high-stakes leadership. Winners are published on Glasscliff and paid.

For working professionals

Turnaround Case Challenge

Write up a real turnaround or crisis role you led or witnessed, anonymised, in 1,200 to 2,000 words: the situation, the conditions you negotiated (or did not), what you did, and what you would do differently.

Judged on: Insight, honesty and usefulness to other leaders.

For students and early-career researchers

Student Research Brief

A 1,500-word brief on a question about the glass cliff, leadership succession or crisis leadership, using published evidence or your own data.

Judged on: Rigour, clarity and originality of the question.

Open to everyone

Leadership Story

A 600 to 1,000-word personal account of stepping into, or deciding against, a high-stakes role. Anonymous entries welcome.

Judged on: Candour, reflection and what others can learn.

Rules

  1. Free to enter. One entry per person per category per round.
  2. Entries must be your own, original and unpublished. Anonymise organisations and people.
  3. Open to adults worldwide, except where prohibited by law.
  4. Entries are judged by the Glasscliff editorial team and, where a round is sponsored, one sponsor-nominated judge with relevant expertise.
  5. Winners are announced within 30 days of the close and paid by bank transfer or PayPal.
  6. By entering you grant Glasscliff a non-exclusive licence to publish your entry with credit (or anonymously, if you ask).
  7. No purchase or payment is required, and supporting Glasscliff does not improve your chances.
  8. We may withhold a prize if no entry meets the standard, and roll the prize into the next round.
