Frequently asked questions

Is the review free?

Requesting a review and the initial conversation are free. If you decide to work with an adviser, you agree their fees directly with them.

Who are the advisers?

Independent executive coaches, employment lawyers, interim executives and turnaround specialists who apply to our Advisor Network and are vetted for relevant experience.

Will my employer find out?

No. We never contact your organisation, and you do not need to name it.

Can an organisation request a review?

Yes. Boards, CHROs and search panels can request a Board Appointment Review for a leader they are appointing or supporting.

Is this legal advice?

Glasscliff does not give legal advice. If you need a contract review, we match you with a qualified employment lawyer.
